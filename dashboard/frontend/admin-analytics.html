How states are determined

Lifecycle describes progress and meaningful activity. Passive visits and refreshes do not count.

Blocked describes a current obstacle to a core action. It is separate from lifecycle.

Commercial value uses settled purchases minus refunds. Admin Grants do not count as purchases.

At risk means 8–29 inactive UTC days. Dormant means at least 30.
